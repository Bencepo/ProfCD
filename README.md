# ProfCD

Profession cooldowns at a glance for WoW Forever. Hover the minimap button to see how long your transmutes, Mooncloth and Salt Shaker still need, for all your characters. Click it to see which crafts you have the mats for.

**Website:** https://bencepo.github.io/profcd/

- **Tooltip on the minimap button.** Every tracked cooldown of every character, with the time left or "Ready".
- **Mats check.** Click the button: a window lists each craft with "Mats in bags", "Mats in bank" or what is missing ("Missing: 1x Arcane Crystal"), and how many crafts the mats are enough for.
- **Forever cooldowns.** Every transmute has its own cooldown (Arcanite, Truesilver, Gold, the elemental transmutes), plus Mooncloth and the Salt Shaker. The durations come from the game, so changes in Forever show up on their own.
- **Recipes from the game.** Reagents are read from the profession window when you open it.
- **Alts included.** Other characters show as of their last login.
- **No minimap button?** Turn it off and ProfCD creates a macro "ProfCD": one click opens the window, the next closes it. `/pcd` works too.
- **Chat notice** when a cooldown of your character is ready.
- **Window of its own:** drag it anywhere, it stays open while you play.

Commands: `/pcd` opens the window, `/pcd minimap` toggles the button, `/pcd forget <name>` removes a character.
